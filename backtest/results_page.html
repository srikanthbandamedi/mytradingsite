Current setup

What the bot runs today

  • Stocks: SNDK, ARM, AMD, PANW, TSLA, CSCO, MRVL, MU, NBIS (fixed list).
  • Buy: MACD above signal, +DI above −DI, ADX ≥ 20, close above VWAP and above the 8:30–8:45 opening range high. When more stocks signal than free slots, the combined score (ADX, relative volume, closeness to VWAP) picks.
  • No new buys while QQQ is below its VWAP, or after 2:55 PM CT.
  • Sell: below VWAP, below opening range low, trend fade (MACD histogram down 2 bars and ADX < 25), or the resting stop order.
  • Stop: placed with every buy. It trails the high close only after the price is 1% above the buy.
  • Positions can be held overnight.
